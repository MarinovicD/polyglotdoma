02 — Try itMini-lesson · 2 minutes

Der, die or das? The last word decides.

German glues words together into long ones. One look tells you their article.

Take two words and glue them together. Which article does the new word get?

derdiedas
das Haushouse+die Türdoor
?dieHaustür
front door

The article comes from the last word.

die Handhand+der Schuhshoe
?derHandschuh
glove
literally: a shoe for your hand
die Handhand+das Tuchcloth
?dasHandtuch
towel
literally: a cloth for your hand
Kraftfahrzeughaftpflicht+dieVersicherung
die Kraftfahrzeughaftpflichtversicherung motor vehicle liability insurance

No matter how long: the last word decides.
