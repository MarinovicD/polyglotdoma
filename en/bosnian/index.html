What brings you to Bosnian?

Every path follows my system, with materials I create for you, one-to-one or in a group. The only difference is where we start and where you want to go.

02 — Try itMini-lesson · 2 minutes

He, she or it? The word tells you.

In English, things have no gender. In Bosnian they do, but most of the time you can see it at a glance. In two minutes, you'll spot it yourself.

In English, a city is simply “it”. In Bosnian, every noun is masculine, feminine or neuter.

masculinefeminineneuter

But you don't have to memorise it. Just take a guess with the next three words, no prior knowledge needed.

Take a guess: what is grad?

gradcity

And rijeka?

rijekariver

And selo?

selovillage

Notice something? Look at the last letter.

gradends in a consonantmasculine
rijekaends in -afeminine
seloends in -o or -eneuter

Your turn. Eight words, just look at the ending.

mostbridge
planinamountain

moresea

dobar means “good”. It follows the gender of the word it describes.

dobarmasculine
dobrafeminine
dobroneuter
dobar danGood daydan is masculine
dobra kafagood coffeekafa is feminine

jutro means “morning”. How do you say good morning?

jutromorning

Dobro jutro!

Now you know why it's dobar dan but dobro jutro: dan is masculine, jutro is neuter.

And from now on, you can tell for almost any new word whether it's masculine, feminine or neuter. In lessons, you build on exactly that, step by step.
